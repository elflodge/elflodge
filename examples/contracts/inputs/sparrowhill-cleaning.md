# Cleaning Services Agreement

This agreement is made between **Sparrowhill Facilities Ltd** (the "Contractor") and **Lantern Analytics Ltd** (the "Client") for the office at 9 Mill Lane, Leeds.

1. **Services.** The Contractor will clean the office each weekday evening as set out in Schedule 1.

2. **Charges.** The Client will pay GBP 1,450 per month, invoiced monthly in arrears. Charges are reviewed each April in line with the Consumer Prices Index.

3. **Term.** This agreement commences on 1 April 2025 and continues for an initial period of two years.

4. **Renewal and termination.** At the end of the initial period this agreement will automatically extend for further periods of one year at a time, unless either party terminates it by giving not less than three months' written notice expiring at the end of the initial period or of any extension.

5. **Termination for breach.** Either party may terminate this agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 14 days of being asked to.

6. **Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.

Schedule 1: daily emptying of bins, vacuuming, kitchen and washroom cleaning; monthly window cleaning.
