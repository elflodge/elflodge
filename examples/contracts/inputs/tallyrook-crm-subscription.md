## 1. Subscription term

The subscription starts on 1 December 2025 and runs for twelve (12) months (the "Initial Term").

## 2. Renewal

This Order Form renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term. Renewal Terms are billed at the list price in effect at renewal.

## 3. Payment

Fees are invoiced annually in advance and are due within thirty (30) days of invoice.

## 4. Data

On termination, Customer may export its data for thirty (30) days, after which Provider deletes it.
