# Equipment Lease Agreement No. EL-2024-0715

**Lessor:** Northline Office Equipment Finance Ltd
**Lessee:** Lantern Analytics Ltd

**Equipment:** two A3 colour multifunction printers, serial numbers on Schedule A.

**Rentals:** 36 monthly rentals of GBP 189.00 plus VAT, payable by direct debit.

**Term:** The lease commences on 15 July 2024 and continues for the minimum period of 36 months. It ends on the last day of the minimum period, when the Lessee shall return the Equipment to the Lessor at the Lessee's cost. There is no option to extend or renew this lease, and the Lessee may not terminate it before the end of the minimum period.

**Service:** Toner and maintenance are included up to 4,000 pages per month per device. Pages over the allowance are charged at GBP 0.009 (mono) and GBP 0.045 (colour).

**Insurance:** The Lessee shall keep the Equipment insured against loss and damage for its full replacement value.
